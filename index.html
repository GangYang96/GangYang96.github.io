About me

My research focuses on prompt learning, knowledge-enhanced natural language processing, and multimodal misinformation detection. I am interested in how pretrained language models can use task-specific prompts and external knowledge to make better use of limited supervision.

My published work explores automatically generated continuous prompts and knowledge-enhanced AutoPrompt methods. My master’s research also examines prompt learning and associations between textual and visual features for fake news detection.

I am seeking PhD opportunities to further investigate knowledge-aware language models and reliable learning from multimodal evidence.
